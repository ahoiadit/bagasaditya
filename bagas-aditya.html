01 / RESEARCHER

About

Human geography, grounded in fieldwork.

Human geographer specialising in the urban informal economy of Indonesia. I hold a Master of Geography (Second Class Honours, Division A) from the University of Melbourne and a Bachelor of Science from Universitas Gadjah Mada, where I developed strong skills in spatial mapping, analysis, and spatio-temporal modelling.

My work combines quantitative and qualitative methods to study informal livelihoods, economic resilience, and urban governance, with field experience across Yogyakarta and other Indonesian cities. I have published in international journals, including Q1 and Q2 outlets, and now seek to extend this work into doctoral research on measuring informality in the urban South.
